Buffer

Work that is not a release, my own tracks, and things that reached me. None of it is the catalogue, and none of it is on a schedule.

A buffer holds something on its way between two places. That is what this page is for. The catalogue is for records, and a record is a compilation that came out of a room with several people in it. Most of what I touch is not that, and until now there was nowhere for the rest of it.

There is nothing on it yet. The page exists before its contents. That is the wrong way round. I am not putting anything here until there is something to put.

What goes on it

Three kinds of thing, and only one of them is mine.

work by other people

Work by anybody, from anywhere. Somebody sends it to me. That is the whole mechanism. There is no form to fill in. No call, no round. I am not asking anyone for anything.

It stays theirs. I take no licence and claim nothing exclusive. The same file can go anywhere else its maker wants, at the same time, and nobody needs to ask me. It is credited to whoever made it and says nothing else about them.

Being here leads to nothing. It is not a way in, and I am not considering anybody for anything because of it. Records come out of rooms people are invited to. That happens somewhere else.

my own

I build the instruments and I make tracks with them, and that work goes out under my own name. It is not a Zariia release. It is not in the catalogue and it never will be. Because the instruments and the two-channel format are the same either way, a listener cannot tell the difference from the object itself. The credit line has to carry it. Mine will say my name and not the label's.

things that reached me

Links out. Work, writing, recordings, tools. I have been listening and reading in a particular direction, and this is what came to me. That is all a list like this ever is. It is not a survey. Nobody has looked at the whole field. There is no register of who is working in it. I am not in a position to tell you who matters.

what it is not
  • Not the catalogue. Nothing here carries a release number and nothing here is a Zariia record.
  • Not dated. No feed, no archive by month, nothing that reads as a schedule.
  • Not counted. No plays, and nothing ordered in a way that implies a ranking.
  • Not a listing of anybody. I publish work, with the name of whoever made it. Nothing here says what anyone is.

The last one is why this is shaped as a holding page and not as a showcase. The label publishes work and credits makers. It does not publish what people are called.
