the mark

A key, standing.

Three concepts in one mark. The bow is a ring holding a lit aperture — the keyed truth, revealed; the key is the opening, not a lock and not a padlock. The bit is a waveform, the music. And it stands on a ground line rather than floating, which is where the stability comes from.

Drawn as a fine line rather than a gold mass, on a fired-clay field, vignetted so the dark has distance in it instead of sitting flat. One warm light, held — no bloom, no neon, no scattered stars.

Two traps recorded in the file. The shank is a vertical <line>, whose bounding box has zero width — an objectBoundingBox gradient on its stroke degenerates and the shank vanishes silently, leaving a mark that reads as a lamp. It uses gradientUnits="userSpaceOnUse" for that reason. And the field must stay fired clay: an indigo tile reads as a foreign blue square on every surface the brand now uses.

the chord — fired earthenware

Terracotta is the body, not an accent.

From four sources that are one idea — Moroccan zellij and pierced lanterns, Kutchi earthenware, Greek pottery, the matryoshka. All of them are vessels carrying banded decoration, and each opens or lets light through.

Gold is the open song. Terracotta is the sealed channel alight, and it arrives as a mass, never a line. Zellij blue-teal is the cold latent state and the machinery. Sober: colour glows, nothing flares, nothing pulses. Never sRGB white. Never #000.

Mono is for labels, the key field, colophons — never paragraphs. Setting body copy in mono made the label read as a terminal; that was caught on render, not in the code. Antic Didone is thin by stroke contrast rather than a weight axis, which keeps it warm where a hairline sans goes cold. Never geometric sans, including the thin ones (Poppins, Jost, Montserrat, Josefin). Never Fraunces — that is Koher's face.

THE MEANDER

Rules the horizontal registers apart, as on a pot. Ornament and namesake at once. Keep it a rule, never a band — it shipped once full-bleed and loud, and read as a shouting stripe.

usage — the lines the brand must not cross

The Zariia description of itself is legal evidence. The brand voice is bound, not free.

  • Presence open, content keyed. Say plainly what the object is. Never let copy or imagery imply it hides, evades or defeats anyone — covertness would be both false and self-incriminating.
  • One identity per screen. On the home page the masthead carries mark and wordmark, so the navigation carries links only. Elsewhere the navigation carries the lockup, because there it is the way back. Never both.
  • The music is real and avowable. A music label, authored and scene-based — not a security product. Warmth, not secrecy.
  • No authority markers, ever. No badges, ticks, verification marks or official styling. Even the label signature — which does prove origin — must be reported as a harm the people it serves.
  • No Koher elements. No braid, no Q/R/L, no Fraunces. Standalone so a notice attaches here, not there.
  • Do not flatten the medium. No solid fills where there is a wash; the vignette is what gives the dark depth.
