Zariia — thin wordmark specimens

All thin. Set in flat luminous gold rather than the gradient, because hairline strokes wash out against a glowing ground. Each shown large and again at 26px, since the real test of a thin wordmark is whether it survives reduction — favicon, footer, a phone.
