# Historicising Zariia

*Drafted 26 July 2026, after Prayas asked how the project is to be historicised. `the-name.md`
traces a word backwards; this asks a different question. What history is this object an instance
of, and what conditions make it legible now rather than at some other time.*

---

## The operation

Etymology and historicisation get confused because both look backwards. They are not the same
movement. An etymology follows a word to its source and stops when it arrives. Historicising asks
what kind of thing this is, which earlier things it belongs with, and what had to be true of a
particular moment for it to appear at all.

Applied to Zariia the answer is unflattering in a specific way, and I think the unflattering
version is the correct one.

## The thesis: this is a recapitulation

Zariia invents nothing. Every layer of it returns a technique that already existed and was
abandoned. What has changed is the reason for the return. These methods were not suppressed and did
not fail. Better infrastructure arrived and nobody missed them. Zariia reaches for them again
because the better infrastructure, while it exists and works, can be switched off by decision.

That is the whole argument. The rest is the evidence for it.

### Sound that carried more than it seemed to

Around 1979 the Dutch broadcaster NOS began transmitting computer programs over the radio on a
programme called Hobbyscoop. Software was stored on audio cassette anyway, so the audio could simply
be broadcast, and listeners across the country held blank tapes to their radios and recorded it. The
format that made this work across incompatible machines was BASICODE, which let one broadcast serve
a ZX Spectrum, a Commodore 64, a BBC Micro and an Apple II.

In Yugoslavia, Radio Belgrade's *Ventilator 202* did the same, roughly a hundred and fifty
broadcasts between 1983 and 1986, largely for the Galaksija.

What arrived over the air sounded like noise and was a program. The same broadcast reached everyone
in earshot, and only those who knew what it was — and had the tape running — took anything from it.
A record with a sealed channel is that arrangement moved indoors: the same object for everyone, and
a second thing inside it for whoever was told.

### The work that requires convening

The third lineage is not engineering at all.

Cage's *Imaginary Landscape No. 4* was composed in 1951 and premiered in New York on 10 May that
year. Twelve radios, twenty-four performers and a conductor, two players to each set, one turning
the dial and the other handling volume and tone. It runs about four minutes. It was the first of his
pieces built entirely on chance operations, and its content is whatever happens to be on the air in
that city at that hour. The work cannot be stored. It can only be convened.

Lucier's *I Am Sitting in a Room*, 1969, records a spoken passage, plays it back into the room,
records that, and repeats until the room's resonant modes dominate and the speech has eroded into
the room's own signature. The room stops being the container and becomes the content. I have not
verified the wording of the spoken text against a reliable source and so have not quoted it here.

Then Zaireeka in 1997, four discs on four machines, and behind it the Parking Lot Experiments with
dozens of car stereos in a garage.

Historically it is also a position with a seventy-year lineage in which the gathering is the medium
and the work does not exist between gatherings.

### Keyed circulation under restriction

The fourth lineage is the one `zariia.md` already gestures at, and it is worth being precise about
where Zariia departs from it.

Magnitizdat was tape-recorded publishing in the Soviet Union: recordings copied privately and passed
along, resilient largely because owning a tape recorder was not illegal and no state can watch every
kitchen. Roentgenizdat, the "bone music" discs, went further and cut grooves into discarded X-ray
film when proper stock was unavailable. In Iran through 1978 and 1979, cassettes of sermons moved
through mosques, universities and families, crossing between city and countryside faster than a
censor could follow.

In all of these the gate sits at the object. You had to be in the network to get the tape, and once
you held it you heard everything on it.

Zariia moves the gate inward, and this is the departure that matters. The object travels freely and
completely; the key is what passes hand to hand. Possession becomes universal and comprehension
becomes the thing that is held. The closer ancestors for that move are the double-voiced traditions,
Polari and the exoteric surface over an esoteric core, where nothing is hidden and what is gated is
the reading.

## The finding that should not be softened

Every form above was abandoned, and none of them was abandoned because of repression.

BASICODE ended because networks arrived. Cassettes ended because copying got easier everywhere.
Each was superseded by something plainly better, and nobody mourned.

So none of this is being revived because it works better. It is being revived because of what it
does to the people using it. A record you cannot fully hear until somebody hands you the key is not
a technical improvement on a record you can. It is a different social object, and the difference is
the point.

## The tension this leaves

Two histories are available and they do not sit together.

The recapitulation reading makes the project modest. An old technique returns under a specific
political condition, does a narrow job while that condition holds, and is superseded again when it
lifts. On this reading the room is what remains after the good channel is closed, and Zariia is a
fallback with a decent argument.

The convening reading is different in kind. On that account the room was always the better place to
dependence that was already worth refusing. This reading has Cage and Lucier behind it, and it is
much closer to Prayas's own practice.

Both are defensible. They produce different projects, different public copy, and different answers
eventually. It does not have to choose today, and pretending the choice has already been made would
be the more serious error.

## A caution about the method

Lineages found afterwards flatter the thing that finds them.

Zariia did not descend from Cage, or from Hobbyscoop, or from a cassette passed between two people
in Tehran in 1978. It arrived at similar constraints and the family resemblance appeared later. Some
of these resemblances are close enough to be instructive, particularly BASICODE, which is nearly the
same system. None of them is a reason, and none of them is evidence that the design is right.

The same discipline that applies to the three letters in the name applies here. A found resemblance
may be pleasant. It is not an argument.
